# Privacy Policy

## 1. Introduction
Welcome to use "Long Picture Stitching Expert" (hereinafter referred to as "this App"). This Privacy Policy is intended to explain how this App collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information, as well as the rights you have regarding your personal information. Please read and understand this Privacy Policy carefully before using this App.

## 2. Collected Information
### 2.1 Device Information
This App may collect information such as your device model, operating system version, device unique identifier (such as IMEI, IDFA, etc., which will be processed in accordance with relevant privacy laws and collected only when necessary and with your authorization), and device manufacturer. This information is used to ensure the normal operation of the App, perform compatibility adaptation, and count the usage of the App.

### 2.2 Picture - related Information
When you use the picture stitching function of this App, the App will temporarily access the picture files you select for stitching. These picture files are only accessed during your stitching operation, and the App will not retain your picture content after the operation is completed.

### 2.3 Log Information
In order to troubleshoot App failures and optimize App performance, this App may collect relevant log information about your use of the App, including operation records, error logs, etc. These log information do not contain your personal identification information.

## 3. Use of Information
### 3.1 Provide Services
The device information and other data collected by this App are mainly used to provide you with stable and efficient long - picture stitching services to ensure that the App can run normally on your device.

### 3.2 Optimize Experience
Through the collected log information and usage data, we can analyze the usage of the App, find and solve potential problems, and optimize App functions and user experience.

### 3.3 Compliance Requirements
In necessary cases such as required by laws and regulations or investigated by relevant departments, we may provide your relevant information as required.

## 4. Sharing of Information
This App will not share your personal information, including device information, temporary access information related to pictures, and log information, with any third party.

## 5. Storage of Information
### 5.1 Storage Period
This App only stores your relevant information for the period necessary to provide services to you. For example, device information will be stored during the installation and use of the App. When you uninstall the App, the relevant device information will be deleted; the temporary access information related to pictures is only temporarily stored when you perform the stitching operation, and is immediately cleared after the operation is completed; log information will be automatically deleted after a certain period of time according to storage needs and compliance requirements.

### 5.2 Storage Location
Your relevant information is only stored locally on your device and will not be uploaded to any remote server.

### 6.2 Deletion
